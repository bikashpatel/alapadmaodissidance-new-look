A life devoted to Odissi

Purnima Piyali The Guru of Alapadma

At the age of six, Purnima Piyali began learning Odissi and folk dance at RAWA Academy in Odisha. A Visharad in Odissi and a Doordarshan ‘B’ Grade Artist, Purnima has carried the dance to festival stages across India, the USA, Singapore and Malaysia.

A journey that began at the age of six

Milestones

From the first step to centre stage

A life measured in training, performances and honours, and in devotion to a living lineage.

  1. Age sixRAWA Academy, Odisha

    The first steps

    Purnima begins learning Odissi and folk dance at RAWA Academy, the premier cultural institution of Odisha, which had its inception in 1990 and grew on the strength of eminent literati, musicians and artists.

  2. DiscipleshipGuru Shri Bharat Charan Giri

    Under the Guru’s tutelage

    Purnima trains under Guru Shri Bharat Charan Giri, a worthy disciple of the Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das style, and comes to carry its uniqueness into every presentation.

  3. From 2003Festival stages across India

    Onto the festival stage

    The first listed stage, Itikili Mitikili at the Book Fair in Bhubaneswar (2003), opens the way to Utkal Divas celebrations in New Delhi (2004) and Chennai (2005), the International Odissi Dance Festival in Bhubaneswar (2013, 2015) and the Taj Festival in Agra (2015).

  4. 2013–2016USA · Singapore · Malaysia

    International stages

    Odissi travels abroad: to New York, Chicago and Seattle in 2013, to The Temple of Fine Arts in Malaysia and Singapore in 2014 and 2016, and to Seattle, Houston and Palo Alto in 2016.

    Purnima successfully completes ‘Visharad’ in Odissi Dance under Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Mandal, Mumbai.

  6. On screenRegional & national television

    Odissi on television

    Performances on regional TV, National TV in New Delhi, E TV and O TV, and for Doordarshan Kendra in Bhubaneswar and New Delhi.

    By the grace of the Almighty I have been awarded ‘B’ Grade Artist of Doordarshan Kendra Bhubaneswar, Odisha, in the year 2016.

Guru Parampara

The lineage behind every step

In Odissi, a style passes from Guru to shishya like one lamp lighting the next. At Alapadma it flows from the style of Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das, through Guru Shri Bharat Charan Giri, to Purnima.

  1. The Style

    Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das

    The Odissi taught at Alapadma follows the style of Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das, a technique marked by its distinctiveness and subtlety. Two signatures of the style live on in the repertoire.

    • Shabda-Swara-Pata A unique concept introduced by Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das in Odissi dance.
    • Sthayee The unique item conceived and choreographed by Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das.

  2. The Guru

    Guru Shri Bharat Charan Giri

    Guru Shri Bharat Charan Giri stands out among the leading dance Gurus of contemporary times. A worthy disciple of the Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das style, he creates choreography that bears unmistakable testimony to the distinctiveness and subtlety of that technique.

    • Prabhat Samgiita

      His dance compositions on Prabhat Samgiita are eloquent about his creative improvisations and have earned him rare recognition at national and international festivals.

    • Shabda-Swara-Patas

      He has enriched the style a great deal through the innovative application of several Shabda-Swara-Patas in his choreographies.

    • Junior & Senior Fellowships

      Awarded by the Government of India in recognition of his talent.

    • Nurturing students

      His industry and personal attention in nurturing the budding talents of his students cannot be overemphasised.

  3. The Disciple

    Purnima Piyali

    Every dance presentation of mine bears testimony to the uniqueness of the Odissi style improvised by Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das.

    Trained under Guru Shri Bharat Charan Giri, Purnima now shares this heritage with students at Alapadma Odissi Dance Academy.

Signature Repertoire

The heart of the repertoire

From the invocation of Mangalacharan to the liberation of Mokshya, an Odissi recital unfolds in five stages, joined in Purnima’s repertoire by the folk dances of Odisha.

I

Invocation

Mangalacharan

An invocatory dance based on Shiva Giitis and Krishna Giitis that opens the recital in salutation.

Explore Mangalacharan
II

Signature of the style

Sthayee

The unique item conceived and choreographed by Guru Shri Deba Prasad Das.

Explore Sthayee
III

Pure dance

Pallavi

Dance that blossoms with the raga: Kalyan, Sankarabharan, Kafi, Basanta and Kalavati.

Explore Pallavi
IV

Expression

Abhinaya

Geeta Govinda, traditional Odissi songs and items on mythological concepts such as Astasambhu, Navarasa, Durga Stuti and Bala Gopalastaka.

Explore Abhinaya
V

Liberation

Mokshya

The final stage of the recital, a dance of spiritual liberation.

Explore Mokshya

Folk dances of Odisha

Sambalpuri & Danda

Two folk dances of Odisha in the repertoire. Purnima began learning folk dance alongside Odissi at the age of six.
